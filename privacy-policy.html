Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy covers the personal automation tools built and operated by Htet Wai Lin ("the developer," "I," "my") that integrate with third-party platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook for the sole purpose of publishing the developer's own content.

What this is

These are personal, single-user automation tools. They are not a public product or service offered to other users. Only the developer operates and authorizes these integrations, using their own accounts on each platform.

What data is accessed

Where an integration requires platform API access (for example, posting a video to a content channel), the tool accesses only the developer's own account data necessary to publish that content — such as video files, titles, and descriptions the developer generates. No data belonging to any other person or account is accessed, collected, or stored.

What data is stored

Authentication tokens required to maintain the integration (for example, OAuth credentials) are stored securely on infrastructure controlled solely by the developer, and are never shared with or accessible to any third party.

What is not collected

This tool does not collect, process, or store personal data from any third-party user. It does not have a public sign-up flow, does not serve other users, and does not use analytics or tracking of any visitors to the connected platforms.
